Verify a Certificate

When an employer calls to confirm a certificate, look it up here by the patient's name (or Certificate No.) and read the real record back to them. This never edits anything.

How employers can check a certificate

Every printed certificate has a QR code. When an employer scans it with a phone, a live page opens and says GENUINE (or CANCELLED) with the patient name, dates and physician. If they call the center instead, search here by the patient's name and read the real record back. Use Cancel in History if a certificate was issued by mistake — its QR page will then show CANCELLED.
